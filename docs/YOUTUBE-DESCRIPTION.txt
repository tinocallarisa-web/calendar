WHAT THIS VISUAL DOES
──────────────────────────────────────────
Most calendar visuals show either KPIs or events — not both. Calendar Events Heatmap combines them:

✅ Heatmap: day cells color by your measure value — instantly see your best and worst days
✅ Event badges: overlay promotions, campaigns, and holidays on the same cell
✅ Categories: up to 8 event types, each with its own color and a built-in legend
✅ Week view: drill into a specific week for a closer look
✅ Cross-page filtering: click a day to filter your entire report

Perfect for retail, FMCG, marketing, and operations — anywhere the question is "what happened on that day, and why?"
