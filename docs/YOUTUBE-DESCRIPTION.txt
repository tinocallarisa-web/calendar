==========================================

A good or bad day usually has a reason on the calendar: a promotion, a campaign, a holiday, a delivery problem. Calendar Events Heatmap puts that reason on the day it happened. Each day is coloured by your measure, and the events sit on the same day as colour marks.

Version 1.2 adds a year view, date-range filtering, 4-4-5 fiscal calendars, color rules, ten colour schemes, five style templates, a setup guide inside the visual, formatting on the canvas and twelve languages.

──────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT IT DOES
──────────────────────────────────────────
✅ Every day coloured by your measure, with a legend for the scale
✅ Promotions, campaigns and incidents as colour marks on their day, one colour per category
✅ Click a category in the legend to hide its events: the day values do not change
✅ Public holidays marked in their own colour, with the name in the tooltip
✅ Year, month and week views; click a day to filter the report, drag for a date range
✅ Fiscal years by month or in 4-4-5, 4-5-4 and 5-4-4 weeks; ISO or US week numbers
✅ Color rules: paint the days above or below your thresholds
✅ Ten colour schemes (two colour-blind safe), five style templates, twelve languages
✅ Bookmarks restore the selected days; keyboard navigation and high contrast supported

Useful in retail, marketing and operations: anywhere the question is "what happened on that day, and why?"
