TESTING THE PREMIUM FEATURES - NO KEY NEEDED
There is no activation key: Pro is granted by the AppSource plan through
IVisualLicenseManager. Import this .pbiviz and open a report in EDIT mode without a
licence: each Pro feature (Year and Week views, 2-12 months, Measure selector, several
events per day, category colours) is drawn WORKING under a "Pro preview" watermark
naming it, a banner names it and the Upgrade bar follows. Reading view shows the free
result. Intentional, not missing gating.

TESTING
1. Date + Main Measure: month heatmap. Add Event Name/Category: colour marks + legend.
   Click a legend category: its marks hide; day values stay.
2. Click a day: other visuals filter. Drag across days (or Shift+click): the report
   filters by the range. Ctrl+click adds; empty space clears.
3. Click the month title: year view (Pro). Click a month name: back to that month.
   Calendar > Week Numbers = ISO, click a week number: week view (Pro). Today button.
4. Calendar > Fiscal Calendar on, start month 10: title shows FY and period.
   Fiscal Periods = 4-4-5: the month view shows a fiscal period of whole weeks.
5. Color Rules: rule 1 >= a value: those days repaint.
6. Edit mode: setup guide with the wells and templates; its "Try Pro" buttons turn a
   Pro view on (watermark). Finish hides it. No fields: start screen with links.
7. Format mode: click a day, its number or the legend to format it on the canvas.
8. Resize to 300x260: compact layout.

WHY NO FX
