WHY THIS SUBMISSION
Replaces 1.1.0.0 (1.1.1.0 was never sent; included here). Purchase notice fixed,
watermarked Pro preview, new brand icon.

PLEASE READ FIRST - PRO PREVIEW
Without a licence, IN EDIT MODE ONLY, Pro options render working under a "Pro preview"
watermark, which the guidelines allow for paid features. Intentional, not missing gating.
In reading view, before the licence resolves, and where licences cannot be read (Publish to
Web, embed, export) the FREE result is drawn with no watermark, so a published report never
uses an unpaid Pro feature.

TESTING - FREE (no active plan)
1. Date + Main Measure: heatmap renders. Add Event Name: 1 badge per day.
2. In EDIT mode click Week: the week view renders under the watermark, a banner names the
   feature and after ~10 s Power BI's Upgrade bar appears. Clicking Week always notifies.
3. Months to Display [Pro] = 3: three months render under the watermark.
4. Switch to reading view: month view, 1 badge per day, no watermark and no notice.
5. Click a day: other visuals filter. Arrows move focus, Enter selects, Escape clears.
6. Resize smaller: all week rows stay visible.

TESTING - PRO (active "calendar-tcviz" plan or its 30-day trial)
1. Week view and 3 months: no watermark, no notice.
2. Event Category Colors apply to badges and legend.

PRIVACY/NETWORK

LICENSING
IVisualLicenseManager only. Pro when the full Service ID ends in ".calendar-tcviz" and state
is Active or Warning; the 30-day trial resolves as Active. Requested once after the first
render, never blocks it; failure stays Free with no prompts. No licensing UI of our own.
